Exhibition

The Dominion
Group Exhibition

Abstract expressionism alongside landscape. Two large paintings sold. A room full of work that surprised me — including my own.

Date
March 2026 Location
The Dominion, San Antonio TX Format
Group Exhibition Medium
Oil, acrylic on canvas

Context

This show came at a specific moment — I'd spent the previous two years making work in Europe, in controlled residency environments with built-in distance from regular life. The Dominion brought that work home. Literally. Into a room where people I know, and people I don't, would walk past it with a glass of wine and make a split-second decision about whether to stop.

That's a different kind of pressure than a white-wall gallery. And it clarified something: the work that stops people isn't always the work you expect it to be.

The Work

I showed a mix — large abstract expressionist canvases and smaller landscape pieces from the Provence and Como residencies. The abstracts outsold the landscapes. That surprised me and didn't.

What I Learned

Abstract work communicates something different in a social setting than landscape does. Landscape asks you to look at a place. Abstract asks you to look at a feeling. In a room where people are already primed to feel something — good wine, good company — the abstract paintings found their people faster.

The titles matter more than I thought. what I didn't say stopped people before they even looked at the painting. That's worth thinking about.

"I don't usually buy art but I kept coming back to it." — collector, after purchasing what I didn't say
