=========================================


you found the lab.

this is ivonne aldaz's personal OS —
a retro Windows desktop built from scratch,
no templates, no no-code tools, no themes.

it started as a portfolio problem. a CV
is a dead document. it sits in a PDF,
gets skimmed for 30 seconds, closes.
i wanted something you could actually
move around in.

so i vibecoded this instead.


START HERE
-----------
new here? open:

  → ask_eve.exe
  → archive.exe
  → notes.app
  → WORK_WITH_ME.exe


WHAT'S INSIDE
--------------
ask_eve.exe
  an AI agent trained on my actual CV.
  ask her anything about the work.
  she'll ask for your email if you seem
  like someone worth knowing.

archive.exe
  10+ years of work. brand strategy,
  art residencies, research reports,
  things i built. organized like a
  real file explorer.

notes.app
  films, books, travel lists, ideas.
  the kind of thing left open on a
  stranger's desktop.

photos.exe
  art and travel. folders, captions,
  lightbox. organized.

snake.exe
  fully playable. global leaderboard.
  come for the work, stay for the game.

leave a note
  anonymous. only i can read them.
  already getting notes from people
  i've never met.


WHAT I ACTUALLY DO
-------------------
brand strategy and creative direction
for founders and growing companies.

i run whitespace — a studio focused
on positioning, narrative, and GTM.
3-month sprints. no retainers.

i also make paintings and ceramics,
write occasionally, and have done
residencies in france and italy.

i grew up on the border.
that shows up in everything.


IS THIS FINISHED?
-----------------
no. it's a living thing.
something new ships, it changes.
some things break. that's part of it.

if something feels strange or broken,
it probably means i was in here
at 2am working on something.
