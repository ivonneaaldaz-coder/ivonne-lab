=========================================
you found the lab.

this is ivonne aldaz's personal OS —
a retro desktop for work, experiments,
art, writing, and assorted side quests.

no template. no theme. no framework.

it started with a portfolio problem:
    
a CV is a dead document.

it sits in a PDF, gets skimmed for 30 seconds,
and closes.

there's no way to ask it a question.
no way to wander.
no way to follow your curiosity.

so i built something you could actually explore.

WHAT'S INSIDE

ask_eve.exe
  an AI agent trained on my resume, projects, and actual work. ask her about marketing, AI, research, art, what i've built, where i've been, or what i'm working on now.
she has boundaries. she also has opinions about being moon-powered.

archive.exe
  12+ years of work. 
research studies. strategy. campaigns.
things i've built. things i've written.
talks i've given. art i've made.

organized like a file explorer, obviously.

notes.app
  books, films, playlists, travel, ideas, resources, and things i wanted to remember.

photos.exe
  art, travel, and whatever currently has my attention.

pulled live from Google Drive.

chatroom.exe
  part Yahoo Chat. part AIM. part internet time machine.

snake.exe
  fully playable. yes, there is a leaderboard. 

leave a note
  send me something.

email is optional —
unless you want me to say hi back.

WHAT I ACTUALLY DO
i work across marketing strategy, research, systems, AI, brand, and creative direction.

sometimes that means helping a company figure out what to say and how to grow.

sometimes it means building the system they need to actually execute it.

i run whitespace, a strategy + creative studio.

i'm also building creative communities and experiences, teaching, making art, writing, and spending an unreasonable amount of time turning ideas into reality.


IS THIS FINISHED?
no.
something new ships, it changes.
some things break.
that's part of the deal.

if something feels strange, unfinished, or slightly unnecessary, i probably added it at 2am because i thought it would be fun.
